# E-Bike Maintenance: What to Service and How Often

An e-bike needs everything a regular bike needs, plus a few extras. Motor torque and extra weight put more load on the drivetrain and brakes, so check those parts sooner than you would on an unassisted bike. The motor and battery add their own care rules, and some jobs belong with a dealer.

![Mechanic checking the chain and drivetrain beside an e-bike motor and battery](/images/articles/e-bike-maintenance.webp)

## What Wears Faster

### Chain and cassette

Added torque stresses the drivetrain, so measure chain wear more often than you would on a regular bike. See [when to replace a bike chain](/articles/when-to-replace-bike-chain) and [how to measure chain wear](/articles/how-to-measure-chain-wear). Replacing a chain on time protects the cassette too.

### Brake pads and rotors

A heavier bike asks more of its brakes. Inspect pads regularly and follow our guide to [when to replace brake pads](/articles/when-to-replace-brake-pads).

### Tires

Motor torque on the rear tire can wear it faster than on an unpowered bike. Our [tire replacement guide](/articles/when-to-replace-bike-tires) covers the wear signs to watch for.

## Battery Care

Follow the manufacturer's guidance for charging and storage, since requirements differ by brand and pack. Avoid leaving the battery in extreme heat or cold. For longer breaks, our [winter bike storage guide](/articles/winter-bike-storage) covers how to store batteries and the rest of the bike.

## Washing an E-Bike

Rinse gently. Do not aim a pressure washer at the motor, battery contacts, or display. Use a low-pressure rinse and a damp cloth near electrical parts, then dry the chain and relube it.

## Leave These to the Dealer

Motor service and firmware updates usually go through an authorized dealer or the manufacturer. When your dealer offers a software update, install it. For how an e-bike's needs compare to other bike types, see [MTB vs road bike maintenance](/articles/mtb-vs-road-maintenance).

## Fit It Into Your Schedule

The same jobs in the [bike maintenance schedule](/articles/bike-maintenance-schedule) apply to an e-bike. The difference is that the drivetrain and brake checks come around sooner.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do e-bikes need more maintenance than regular bikes?

They need the same jobs, and the drivetrain, brakes, and tires often come due sooner because of the added torque and weight.

### Can I wash an e-bike?

Yes, gently. Keep water away from the motor, battery contacts, and display, and avoid high-pressure spray.

### Who should service the motor?

Usually an authorized dealer or the manufacturer. Check your warranty terms before opening anything yourself.

### How should I store the battery?

Follow your manufacturer's instructions for charge level and temperature, especially over the off-season.
