# Bike Wheel Truing: Signs Your Wheel Is Out of True

A wheel is out of true when the rim wobbles side to side or hops up and down as it spins. You can usually spot it in under a minute with a spin test. Check it before you blame your brakes, because a wobbling rim and a bent rotor feel almost the same on the bike.

![Road bike wheel in a workshop stand showing spokes and disc rotor](/images/articles/bike-wheel-truing.webp)

## The Spin Test

Lift the wheel off the ground and spin it. Watch the gap between the rim and a fixed point such as a brake pad, the fork leg, or a chainstay.

- **Lateral wobble:** the gap grows and shrinks as the rim moves side to side.
- **Radial wobble (a hop):** the rim seems to rise and drop as it turns.

A tiny, smooth wobble is common and usually harmless. Anything that touches the brake, fork, or frame needs attention before you ride.

## Rim Rub, Rotor Rub, or Pad Drag?

These three get confused all the time, and truing the wheel will not fix the wrong one.

- **Rubbing once per wheel turn** points to the rim or wheel.
- **Rubbing that stays constant** usually points to pad or caliper alignment.
- **A rhythmic pulse on disc brakes** can come from a bent rotor. See [when to replace brake rotors](/articles/when-to-replace-brake-rotors) for how to check rotor wear and warping.

## Check the Spokes

Squeeze pairs of spokes together around the wheel. A spoke that feels much looser than its neighbors, or one that clicks and pings, means tension has changed. A broken spoke is a shop job, and the wheel should not be ridden hard until it is fixed.

## DIY or Shop?

- **Small lateral wobble with even spoke tension:** a spoke wrench and patience can fix it if you have done it before.
- **Radial hop, broken spokes, or a dented rim:** take it to a shop.
- **A wheel that keeps going out of true:** ask the shop to check spoke tension and rim condition, since repeat wobble points to an underlying problem.

If you are not sure, a shop truing is a quick service. It also makes a good add-on when you are already paying for a [tune-up](/articles/bike-tune-up-cost).

## When to Replace the Wheel

A cracked or deeply dented rim cannot be trued back to safe. Replace it. For a sense of how wheels and other parts age, see [how long bike parts last](/articles/how-long-do-bike-parts-last).

## Make It a Habit

Spin each wheel as part of your [pre-ride bike check](/articles/pre-ride-bike-check), and again after any hard impact such as a pothole or a crash. The earlier you catch a wobble, the easier it is to fix.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I check wheel trueness?

Spin each wheel during your pre-ride check and after any hard impact. It takes a few seconds.

### Can I ride on a slightly wobbly wheel?

A light wobble that clears the brake and frame is common. If the rim touches anything, or the wobble is getting worse, get it fixed before you ride.

### Is it the wheel or the rotor?

If the rub happens once per wheel turn and follows the rim, suspect the wheel. If the brake pulses or you can see the rotor bend, suspect the rotor.

### When is a wheel not worth truing?

A cracked or dented rim, or a wheel with several broken spokes, is usually a replacement job.
